I lost 80% of my portfolio since I traded. Today is the 5th November. It has been a while since I opened my IBKR app. I'm left with less than 10k USD. I wasn't sure what to feel. Sadness, disappointment, anxiety.... a blend of emotions well up within my chest and attempted to force out tears from my eyes. I held back. Months and months of savings and hard work, all gone. I'm writing this in hopes to relieve some of my "feelings".

I started trading around 2024 when I was doing my masters. Back then I had a bit of savings. I dabbled in Nvidia options. Nvidia was on huge momentum, priced around 800 USD. Indeed, I quickly tripled my capital in a short span of time. It was nerve-racking. I wasn't satisfied though. I continue to purchasing options, but soon a small correction to the stock came, before the stock split. I lost all my money as my options went to 0. I learnt not to touch options since then. (A rule that I'm trying very hard to obey).

Next I attempted to mimic Warren Buffett. I did a bit of research on his holdings and used Gemini to analyze these stocks. With these understanding, I bought Japanese stocks, e.g. Itochu, Marubeni and the other Sogo Shosha. I gain roughly 40% in close to a year using leverage. But I soon wasn't satisfied. At around the same time, AI stocks were skyrocketing. Every stocks related to AI had a huge boost in price. I saw postings on 小红书 and people were posting profits of few hundred percent in a few months. Sandisk had a steady momentum from 200 to 2000 within a year! It seems like it will continue rising. I FOMOed.

I sold part of my Japanese stocks and bought Micron at 200. It went to 350 in a while and I gained a couple thousands. I thought to myself, easy money; I sold my Japanese shares and bought a bit of DRAM ETF at a price of around 52 bucks.

It went up to 62 soon. I was stoked. I continued to sell all my Japanese shares and went all in on DRAM ETF. It shot up to 80!!! I was over the moon. I told my friends about it and shared my conviction. Things were looking good. Seemed like this stock would rise until 100, just like many who said on 小红书.

Democrats won the midterm elections, investors questioned the payoff of AI investment. Thus, stocks have been volatile these few months. As usual, the fear of losing money gripped me. I tried not to open my IBKR app. When I did, I started questioning my decisions, and when I get home from work I would review my thesis again and would again encourage myself.

Soon, things fell apart. As with cyclical stocks, this stock began to drop as investors worry about oversupply. It was then I knew of the term "cyclical". I read up on it, did a deep dive on memory stocks. I held firm to my conviction during these volatile period. My feelings rose and dropped as with the stock price. Today it hit 43 premarket and I started questioning myself. I lose faith.

Granted, I could be right, but still lose money. Should I continue holding? Should I sell? I do not know.

Reflecting back, these few years of my trading experience stripped me bare and naked to reveal the hidden desires of my heart, especially in uncertain times. I'm forced to face my own greed during the boom, my own fear during recession or correction, and the choices I made accompanying these moments. I'm ashamed. I didn't know I had these emotions within me. My lack of self control, my desire to get rich fast was the fuel behind these risk taking trades.

Now I'm faced with my own mediocrity in the mirror. At 28, I have 10k USD, summing both my savings and investment (yes I invested all my savings). I learnt that the boring and slow way of getting rich is the safer path. Deep down in my heart, I couldn't accept it, not even now. I could only control my action and try to be logical.

The Lord is close to the broken-hearted. Maybe God is trying to teach me to focus more on Him as a provider instead of money. I'm trying to turn to Him, even as my heart couldn't accept it.
